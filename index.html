Principled trust in cooperative autonomy.

We study how connected and automated vehicles can perceive, communicate, and act with quantifiable safety guarantees, from intersection-scale coordination to the integrity of a single perception estimate.

Director: Prof. Eunjeong Hyeon Based at CE-CERT, UC Riverside

About

Safe, Human-centric, Intelligent, Future Technology

SHIFT Lab designs and validates cooperative driving automation (CDA) systems that hold up under uncertainty: unreliable sensors, contested right-of-way, and communication that arrives late or not at all. Our work sits at the intersection of multi-agent control, V2X communication, and statistical guarantees for perception.

Autonomy earns trust not by acting confidently, but by knowing precisely when it should not.

That thesis grounds three lines of work: integrity monitoring for perception, agreement-seeking coordination among connected vehicles, and testbeds that let both be measured against real traffic, not just simulation.

Research

Three problems we return to

Each project below is a different angle on the same question: what can a cooperative autonomous system prove about its own decisions, and what should it do when it can't.

Perception Protection Level (PPL)

A GNSS RAIM-inspired integrity monitoring framework for CAV perception, built on conformal prediction to bound per-estimate localization error against an explicit alert limit. Grounded in ISO 26262 and ISO 21448 (SOTIF), and validated on nuScenes for 3D object localization.

In development

Agreement-Seeking Cooperative Driving

AI-driven, agreement-based coordination for connected automated vehicles, spanning message-level protocol design (ASM/ISM/BSM), perception-accuracy evaluation, and on-track validation of full-stack decision-making under real vehicle-in-the-loop conditions.

On-track validation underway

Intersection Safety & Smart Testbeds

Infrastructure and instrumentation for intersection-scale safety research, including a proposed "mini city" testbed at CE-CERT for repeatable, instrumented evaluation of cooperative driving systems beyond what simulation alone can validate.

Proposal stage
